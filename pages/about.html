the project
sherboa engine is a free, independent project created for educational and experimental purposes.
it is designed as a simple tool for comparing video quality with a focus on video engineering, streaming and media systems.

powered by linux & all his friends

VMAF
the industry's preferred metric, is an open-source perceptual video quality assessment algorithm developed by netflix.
however, please note sherboa engine is not affiliated with or endorsed by netflix.

SSIM
evaluates how visually similar a compressed or processed video is to the original.
instead of comparing pixels individually, it measures similarities in luminance, contrast, and structural details, making it more representative of human visual perception.

PSNR
measures the mathematical difference between an original video and a compressed or processed version.
it is based on pixel-by-pixel error and quantifies how much distortion has been introduced during encoding or processing.
unlike SSIM, which focuses on perceived visual similarity, PSNR focuses on signal accuracy and reconstruction error.

ffmpeg
is a free and open-source multimedia framework for video processing and media analysis.

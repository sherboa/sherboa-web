how does this work?

the goal is simple: reference video + distorted video -> analysis -> quality metrics
sherboa engine provides: VMAF mean - max - min, PSNR, SSIM, .JSON/.XML/.CSV files, and more!
here's an example of what you may upload:

reference video frame: quality baseline

original high resolution-uncompressed 1080p 25fps source video

distorted video frame, under evaluation

transcoded high compressed-cropped 480p 25fps video

for meaningful results, make sure the reference and distorted videos should represent the same content and be temporally alligned.

keep in mind that VMAF is a perceptual quality metric, not an absolute measurement of video quality.
use it together with technical analysis and other relevant metrics whenever evaluating encoding/transcoding performance.
